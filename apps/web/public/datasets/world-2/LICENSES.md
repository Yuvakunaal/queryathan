# World 2 dataset licenses

All three files are **CC0** (public domain dedication), synthetic, and
generated by [`scripts/generate-vault.mjs`](../../../../scripts/generate-vault.mjs)
with a seeded deterministic PRNG. Names, emails and numbers are invented;
phone numbers use the reserved 555-01xx range. Regenerate with
`node scripts/generate-vault.mjs`.

- `pin-tumbler.csv`: 150 rows, `contact_id, name, phone, city`. Phone numbers in seven formats.
- `latin-lock.csv`: 160 rows, `customer_id, customer, city, comment`. UTF-8 text mis-decoded as Latin-1 in about a third of cells.
- `warden.csv`: 180 rows, `member_id, name, email, phone`. Mojibake, messy emails and messy phones together.
