# World 8 dataset licenses

All files are **CC0** (public domain dedication), synthetic, and generated by
[`scripts/generate-timekeeper.mjs`](../../../../scripts/generate-timekeeper.mjs) with a seeded deterministic PRNG. Names,
ids, dates and amounts are invented. Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-timekeeper.mjs`; the same script
writes the case files and their expected answers, so data and answer key cannot drift apart.

- `events.csv`: 60 rows, `event_id, raw_date`; three spellings of a date.
- `shipments.csv`: 70 rows, `order_id, ordered, shipped`.
- `store-orders.csv`: 200 rows, `order_id, store, utc_ts`; timestamps are UTC.
- `store-offsets.csv`: 6 rows, `store, utc_offset_minutes`; minutes east of UTC.
- `sparse-sales.csv`: 42 rows, `day, revenue`; days with no sales are missing, not zero.
- `price-orders.csv`: 90 rows, `order_id, product, ordered`.
- `price-history.csv`: 32 rows, `product, effective_date, price`; a new row each time a price changes.
- `bookings.csv`: 52 rows, `room, start_ts, end_ts`; all on one day, written YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM.
