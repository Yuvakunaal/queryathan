# World 3 dataset licenses

All eight files are **CC0** (public domain dedication), synthetic, and generated by
[`scripts/generate-twins.mjs`](../../../../scripts/generate-twins.mjs) with a seeded
deterministic PRNG. Names, ids and amounts are invented. Regenerate with
`node scripts/generate-twins.mjs`.

Each case has an `-orders.csv` (`order_id, customer_id, item, amount`) and a
`-customers.csv` (`customer_id, customer_name, city`):

- `key-mirror`: 120 orders, 40 customers, clean keys.
- `ghost-twin`: 130 orders with stray spaces and mixed case in `customer_id`; 45 customers.
- `double-vision`: 120 orders; 40 customers with 12 rows listed twice.
- `the-twins`: 150 orders with messy keys and 9 orphans; 50 customers with 10 repeated rows.

Multi-table joins, generated by [`scripts/generate-twins-multi.mjs`](../../../../scripts/generate-twins-multi.mjs)
(also CC0 and synthetic; the same script writes the case files and their expected answers):

- `three-way-*.csv`: orders (60), customers (22), products (8).
- `four-corners-*.csv`: orders (80), customers (26), products (8), stores (5).
