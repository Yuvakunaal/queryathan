# World 9 dataset licenses

All files are **CC0** (public domain dedication), synthetic, and generated by
[`scripts/generate-laboratory.mjs`](../../../../scripts/generate-laboratory.mjs) with a seeded deterministic PRNG. Names,
ids, dates and amounts are invented. Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-laboratory.mjs`; the same script
writes the case files and their expected answers, so data and answer key cannot drift apart.

- `measurements.csv`: 120 rows, `sample_id, lab, value`.
- `sensor-readings.csv`: 200 rows, `reading_id, sensor, value`.
- `blood-pressure.csv`: 90 rows, `patient_id, ward, bp`; about a fifth of bp is missing.
- `customers-by-age.csv`: 100 rows, `customer_id, age, spend`.
- `experiment.csv`: 1850 rows, `user_id, variant, converted`; converted is 1 or 0.
- `dose-response.csv`: 115 rows, `run_id, compound, dose, response`; some responses are missing.
