# Sound licenses

`key-1.wav` to `key-8.wav` are single key presses taken from "Keyboard Soundpack #1
[Typing and Single Keystrokes]" by Unicae Games, released under **CC0** (public domain):
https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes

Recorded on a Cherry KC 1000 with a Shure SM7B. The only changes made here are cutting each
to 0.2 seconds with a short fade-out and levelling the volume. The tones for running code,
errors and winning are synthesized in the browser (`src/lib/sound.ts`) and are not files.

`slice-1.wav` is a knife attack from "20 sword sound effects (attacks and
clashes)" by StarNinjas, released under **CC0**:
https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes

Converted from Ogg to WAV, levelled, and trimmed to the end of the sound; nothing else changed.
